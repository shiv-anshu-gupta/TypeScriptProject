Questions

Good to know

Do you deliver to my home?

No. You collect your order at the shop. The app saves you the waiting: your bag is packed and ready when you arrive.

Is the price in the app final?

It is an estimate the shop prepares for you. Your final bill is made at the counter. If you paid by UPI and the bill comes out lower, the difference is returned; if it would be higher, you are told before being asked for more.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes - from the app, until the shop starts packing. After that, message or call the shop. There is never a cancellation charge. See our Refund Policy.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free to download and free to use. You only pay for your groceries.

What happens to the photo of my list?

It is read once to turn it into text, then thrown away - it is not saved with your order. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
