Write your grocery list. Your kirana shop gets it ready.

sKirana lets you send your grocery list to your local kirana shop from your phone. The shop tells you the total, packs your order, and lets you know when it is ready. You collect it and pay at the counter - no online payment needed.

चार आसान कदम

How sKirana works, from your list to your bag.

लिस्ट लिखें

Write your list

Type items the way you say them - "आटा 5 किलो" or "sugar 1 kg".

भेजें

Send it

One tap sends the list straight to the shop.

दाम देखें

See the total

The shop prices every item, so you know the bill before you go.

दुकान से लें

Collect & pay

Get a note when it is packed. Pick it up and pay at the counter.

sKirana क्यों?

Made for the way families already shop at their kirana store.

हिंदी या EnglishYour language

Use the whole app in Hindi or English, and write your list in either.

पहले दाम, फिर खरीदPrice first

See what your order will cost before you leave home. No surprises at the counter.

हर अपडेट फ़ोन परOrder updates

A notification when your list is priced, packed and ready to collect.

पेमेंट दुकान परPay at the shop

No card details in the app. Pay in cash or by UPI when you collect.
